# Spike E: test sheet

Fill in the times with a phone stopwatch, then copy them into the results table in `PLAN.md` (section 8b).

**Before you start:** restart the Kobo. Do the whole sheet in one sitting, top to bottom.

**For each book:** open it, wait for the page count, then time:

- **TOC jump:** Table of contents → **INDEX** (the last entry). Stop at **120 s** and write "120+".
- **Page jump:** jump to about **75%**.
- **10 page turns:** time all ten together.

If the reader crashes to home, write the time it happened and "yes" under Crash, then reopen the book
for the next test.
